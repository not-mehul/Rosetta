# A short guide to Rosetta

Rosetta is a quiet place to read. It keeps your books, papers and notes on this device, remembers where you stopped, and stays out of the way while you read.

## What it reads

- **EPUB**, the format most ebooks come in, with its chapters, contents and images.
- **PDF**, page by page, with text you can select, search and highlight.
- **Word** documents (`.docx`), with their headings, lists, tables and pictures.
- **Markdown**, **HTML** and **plain text**, set in the same type as everything else.

Add files with the **+** button, drop them on the window, or choose *Open with Rosetta* from another app. Adding the same file twice keeps one copy.

## While you read

Tap the middle of the page to hide the toolbars, and again to bring them back. Scrolling down hides them too.

**Contents** lists the chapters, your bookmarks and your highlights. Tap any of them to go there.

**Search** looks through the whole document. Tap a result and the words flash where they are.

**Bookmarks** mark the place on screen. Tap the bookmark at the top again to remove it.

**Highlights** start with a selection. Choose a colour from the bar that appears, or *Note* to write something beside it. Tap a highlight later to change its colour, edit the note or remove it.

## Type and theme

The **Aa** button sets the typeface, size, line spacing and width of the column, and whether lines are justified. For PDFs it sets the zoom and whether pages keep their paper or take on the Dusk theme.

Rosetta has two themes. *Dusk* is a late-night reading room and *Dawn* is a sunlit study. *System* follows your device.

## Your notes

From a document's details, or from the highlights list, you can export your highlights and notes as Markdown. Settings can back up every place, bookmark and highlight to a file, and restore them later.

*Private.* Nothing you add leaves this device.
