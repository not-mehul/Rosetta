# A short guide to Rosetta

Rosetta is a quiet place to read. It keeps your books, papers and notes on this device, remembers where you stopped, and stays out of the way while you read.

## What it reads

- **EPUB**, the format most ebooks come in, with its chapters, contents and images.
- **PDF**, page by page, with text you can select, search and highlight.
- **Word** documents (`.docx`), with their headings, lists, tables and pictures.
- **OpenDocument** (`.odt`), **RTF** and **FictionBook** (`.fb2`).
- **Comic archives** (`.cbz`), one picture after another.
- **Markdown**, **HTML** and **plain text**, set in the same type as everything else.

Add files with the **+** button, a whole folder at once (a Calibre library brings its titles, series, tags and covers), drop them on the window, or choose _Open with Rosetta_ from another app. On Android, text shared to Rosetta from another app, such as a passage from a browser or an email, is kept as a document. Adding the same file twice keeps one copy. The **Catalogs** tab has free books from Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks, and can add a Calibre server or a library's catalog (OPDS 1 or 2). A catalog's own choices, such as language or order, show as chips above its books, and a book you already have says _In your library_.

Books bought with DRM can't be opened here. Read them in the app they were bought for, or look for a DRM-free copy.

## While you read

Tap the middle of the page to hide the toolbars, and again to bring them back. Scrolling down hides them too.

In **Aa**, the **Page** tab offers **Flow**: one sentence at a time, letter by letter. Tap to show the rest of the sentence and again for the next; tap the left edge to go back, and the count under the sentence to bring the toolbars back. **Visual anchors** set the start of every word in bold, and **Yours** reads in a font file of your own.

In **Pages** mode (also in the **Page** tab), tap the left or right edge, swipe, or use the arrow keys to turn a page. The corner curls under your finger; choose _Slide_ or _None_ if you prefer less motion. On Android the volume keys turn pages too. On a tablet held sideways, pages lie open two at a time, as a printed book does; turn _Two pages side by side_ off in the **Page** tab for one. Comics marked as manga read right to left.

Tap a footnote number and the note opens over the page. Tap a picture to see it full screen.

**Read aloud**, in the **Listen** tab under **Aa**, reads from the place on screen, sentence by sentence, and shows each sentence as it goes. It carries on with the screen off, and a sleep timer stops it after a while or at the end of the chapter.

**Contents** lists the chapters, your bookmarks and your highlights. Tap any of them to go there.

**Search** looks through the whole document. Tap a result and the words flash where they are.

After you jump somewhere from the contents, a search, a link or a note, **Back to** appears in the footer and returns you to where you were. Tap the time left in the footer to see the time left in the chapter, or which chapter you are in.

**Bookmarks** mark the place on screen. Tap the bookmark at the top again to remove it.

**Highlights** start with a selection. Choose a colour from the bar that appears, or _Note_ to write something beside it. Tap a highlight later to change its colour, edit the note, share it as a card or remove it. _Look up_ asks the dictionary.

## Type and theme

The **Aa** button opens the reading panel over the page, so you can watch each change as you make it. Its tabs show only what fits the document:

- **Text** sets the typeface, size, line spacing and width of the column, and whether lines are justified.
- **Page** chooses scrolling, pages or Flow, and for PDFs the zoom and whether pages keep their paper. A PDF can also show as **Text**: its words laid out in your type, for reading a paper on a phone, in any mode and aloud. Highlights and places carry over between the two.
- **Light** has the theme, _Dim_, _Warmth_ and _Keep the screen on_.
- **Listen** reads aloud, at the speed and in the voice you choose.

Each book keeps the type and page settings changed while it is open, and a new book starts from the defaults. _Use for all books_ makes one book's settings the defaults; _Back to the defaults_ drops them.

Rosetta has two themes. _Dusk_ is a late-night reading room and _Dawn_ is a sunlit study. _System_ follows your device. The theme is also in Settings.

## Your library

The tabs under the title are **Library**, **Notes**, **Stats** and **Catalogs**. In the Library, **Arrange** chooses covers or a list, the order, and which collection or tag to show. Search the library with the magnifier: it finds titles and authors, and under _Inside the books_ the places in every book where the words appear (the first search reads each book once). Search with `#tag` for a tag. Tap a book's **⋮** for its details, where you can put it in a **collection**, and _Edit details_ fixes the title, author, series, tags and cover.

**Stats** shows time read today and this week, your streak, a calendar of the last few months and what you finished this year. Set a daily goal there, or a number of books for the year, if you'd like one. The first time the library opens in a new week, it shows last week's reading until you put it away; when nothing is in progress, it shows a line from your highlights instead.

## Your notes

The **Notes** tab gathers every highlight from every document; filter it by colour or words, and _Export these_ saves what it shows as Markdown. A book's details have room for a note on the whole book, which goes out with its highlights. From a document's details, or from the highlights list, you can export them as Markdown.

Settings can bring over a Kindle's highlights from its _My Clippings.txt_, onto the same words in the same books here. It can also back up every place, bookmark, highlight and collection to a file, save the whole library with its files, and restore either later. **Sync with a folder** keeps places and notes the same on your devices through a folder you share between them: choose one in iCloud Drive, or one Syncthing or Dropbox keeps in step. Weekly backups can go to a folder of your choice too. **Storage** shows what each file takes and can offload one, keeping its place and notes until you add it again.

A finished book can be logged in **BookShelf**, with your note on it, and a highlight can go there as a quote from the highlight itself. An EPUB can be opened in **Colophon**.

_Private._ Nothing you add leaves this device unless you open a catalog, sync to a folder you chose, or share it.
