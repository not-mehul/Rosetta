# A short guide to Rosetta

Rosetta is a quiet place to read. It keeps your books, papers and notes on this device, remembers where you stopped, and stays out of the way while you read.

## What it reads

- **EPUB**, the format most ebooks come in, with its chapters, contents and images.
- **PDF**, page by page, with text you can select, search and highlight.
- **Word** documents (`.docx`), with their headings, lists, tables and pictures.
- **OpenDocument** (`.odt`), **RTF** and **FictionBook** (`.fb2`).
- **Comic archives** (`.cbz`), one picture after another.
- **Markdown**, **HTML** and **plain text**, set in the same type as everything else.

Add files with the **+** button, drop them on the window, or choose _Open with Rosetta_ from another app. Settings can add a whole folder at once, and a Calibre library brings its titles, series, tags and covers. On Android, text shared to Rosetta from another app, such as a passage from a browser or an email, is kept as a document. Adding the same file twice keeps one copy.

Books bought with DRM can't be opened here. Read them in the app they were bought for, or look for a DRM-free copy.

## While you read

Tap the middle of the page to hide the toolbars, and again to bring them back. Scrolling down hides them too.

In **Aa**, the **Page** tab offers **Flow**: one sentence at a time, letter by letter. Tap to show the rest of the sentence and again for the next; tap the left edge to go back, and the count under the sentence to bring the toolbars back. Hold a sentence to highlight it. In scroll mode, _Start auto-scroll_ (in the **Page** tab) moves the page up at the pace set in Settings: tap to pause and go on.

In **Pages** mode (also in the **Page** tab), tap the left or right edge, swipe, or use the arrow keys to turn a page. The corner curls under your finger; Settings can make pages slide or turn without motion instead, and on Android lets the volume keys turn them. On a tablet held sideways, pages lie open two at a time, as a printed book does. Comics marked as manga read right to left.

Tap a footnote number and the note opens over the page. Tap a picture to see it full screen.

**Read aloud**, in the **Listen** tab under **Aa**, reads from the place on screen, sentence by sentence, and shows each sentence as it goes. It carries on with the screen off, and a sleep timer stops it after a while or at the end of the chapter.

**Contents** lists the chapters, your bookmarks and your highlights. Tap any of them to go there.

**Search** looks through the whole document. Tap a result and the words flash where they are.

After you jump somewhere from the contents, a search, a link or a note, **Back to** appears in the footer and returns you to where you were. Tap the time left in the footer to see the time left in the chapter, or which chapter you are in.

**Bookmarks** mark the place on screen. Tap the bookmark at the top again to remove it.

**Highlights** start with a selection. Choose a colour from the bar that appears, or _Note_ to write something beside it. Tap a highlight later to change its colour, edit the note, share it as a card or remove it with the bin. _Look up_ asks the dictionary.

## The reading panel

The **Aa** button opens the reading panel over the page, so you can watch each change as you make it. Its tabs show only what fits the document:

- **Text**: Serif or Sans, the size, line spacing, justified lines, and the book's original style.
- **Page**: scrolling, pages or Flow, and keeping the screen on. A PDF has its zoom and fit here, and can show as **Text**: its words laid out in your type, for reading a paper on a phone, in any mode and aloud. Highlights and places carry over between the two.
- **Light**: true black for OLED screens, and night light, which dims and warms the page together in five steps.
- **Listen**: read aloud, at the speed and in the voice you choose.

Each book keeps the text and page settings changed while it is open. The arrow at the top of the panel puts it back on the defaults.

## Settings

Settings, at the top right of the library, has five tabs:

- **General**: the theme (_Dusk_ is a late-night reading room, _Dawn_ a sunlit study, _System_ follows your device), the language, a daily goal and a number of books for the year, a reminder to read at a time you choose, and the library's storage.
- **Reading**: the defaults every book starts with, from the same controls as the reading panel, with Flow's speed, auto-scroll's pace and how pages turn. Under _Advanced_: the reading ruler (a band over the line you're reading), visual anchors (the start of each word in bold), the width of the column, and two pages side by side.
- **Listen**: the speed and voice for reading aloud.
- **Data**: back up now, with or without the books' files, and restore. Choose a folder that iCloud Drive, Syncthing or Dropbox keeps the same on your devices, and Rosetta syncs places, highlights and notes through it and writes automatic backups there, daily, weekly or monthly. _Clear all data_ starts again from nothing, after asking.
- **About**: the version, and the easter eggs found.

## Your library

The tabs under the name are **Library**, **Notes** and **Stats**. Each book in the Library carries a small mark: a dashed ring if it hasn't been opened, an open book if you're reading it, a tick once it's finished. The search field finds titles and authors, and under _Inside the books_ the places in every book where the words appear (the first search reads each book once). Search with `#tag` for a tag. The filter beside it shows only what's unopened, being read or finished, and chooses covers or a list, the order, and a collection or tag. Tap a book's **⋮** for its details, where you can put it in a **collection**, and _Edit details_ fixes the title, author, series, tags and cover.

**Stats** shows time read today and this week, your streak, how far along your goals are, a calendar of the last few months and what you finished this year. The first time the library opens in a new week, it shows last week's reading until you put it away; when nothing is in progress, it shows a line from your highlights instead.

## Your notes

The **Notes** tab gathers every highlight and bookmark from every document, under each book's title. Search finds words, notes and chapters in both; the chips show bookmarks only, or one colour. Tap any of them to open the book at that place. A book's details have room for a note on the whole book, and from there, or from a book's highlights list, its notes export as Markdown.

**Storage**, in Settings, shows what each file takes and can offload one, keeping its place and notes until you add it again.

A finished book can be logged in **BookShelf**, with your note on it, and a highlight can go there as a quote from the highlight itself.

_Private._ Nothing you add leaves this device unless you sync to a folder you chose, or share it.
