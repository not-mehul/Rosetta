# A short guide to Rosetta

Rosetta is a quiet place to read. It keeps your books, papers and notes on this device, remembers where you stopped, and stays out of the way while you read.

## What it reads

- **EPUB**, the format most ebooks come in, with its chapters, contents and images.
- **PDF**, page by page, with text you can select, search and highlight.
- **Word** documents (`.docx`), with their headings, lists, tables and pictures.
- **OpenDocument** (`.odt`), **RTF** and **FictionBook** (`.fb2`).
- **Comic archives** (`.cbz`), one picture after another.
- **Markdown**, **HTML** and **plain text**, set in the same type as everything else.

Add files with the **+** button, a whole folder at once, drop them on the window, or choose _Open with Rosetta_ from another app. Adding the same file twice keeps one copy. **Catalogs** (the globe) has free books from Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks, and can add a Calibre server or a library's catalog.

Books bought with DRM can't be opened here. Read them in the app they were bought for, or look for a DRM-free copy.

## While you read

Tap the middle of the page to hide the toolbars, and again to bring them back. Scrolling down hides them too.

In **Aa**, Reading offers **Flow**: one sentence at a time, letter by letter. Tap to show the rest of the sentence and again for the next; tap the left edge to go back, and the count under the sentence to bring the toolbars back. **Visual anchors** set the start of every word in bold, and **Yours** reads in a font file of your own.

In **Pages** mode (under **Aa**), tap the left or right edge, swipe, or use the arrow keys to turn a page. The corner curls under your finger; choose _Slide_ or _None_ if you prefer less motion. On Android the volume keys turn pages too.

Tap a footnote number and the note opens over the page. Tap a picture to see it full screen.

**Read aloud** reads from the place on screen, sentence by sentence, and shows each sentence as it goes.

**Contents** lists the chapters, your bookmarks and your highlights. Tap any of them to go there.

**Search** looks through the whole document. Tap a result and the words flash where they are.

After you jump somewhere from the contents, a search, a link or a note, **Back to** appears in the footer and returns you to where you were. Tap the time left in the footer to see the time left in the chapter, or which chapter you are in.

**Bookmarks** mark the place on screen. Tap the bookmark at the top again to remove it.

**Highlights** start with a selection. Choose a colour from the bar that appears, or _Note_ to write something beside it. Tap a highlight later to change its colour, edit the note, share it as a card or remove it. _Look up_ asks the dictionary.

## Type and theme

The **Aa** button sets the typeface, size, line spacing and width of the column, and whether lines are justified. For PDFs it sets the zoom and whether pages keep their paper or take on the Dusk theme.

Rosetta has two themes. _Dusk_ is a late-night reading room and _Dawn_ is a sunlit study. _System_ follows your device. _Dim_ and _Warmth_ soften the screen at night, and _Keep the screen on_ stops it sleeping while you read.

## Your library

Put documents on **collections** with the chips above the shelves, or from a document's details, where _Edit details_ also fixes the title, author, series, tags and cover. Search the library with `#tag`. **Reading** (the chart) shows time read today and this week, your streak, a calendar of the last few months and what you finished this year. Set a daily goal there if you'd like one.

## Your notes

**All notes** (the highlighter) gathers every highlight from every document. From a document's details, or from the highlights list, you can export them as Markdown.

Settings can back up every place, bookmark, highlight and collection to a file, save the whole library with its files, and restore either later. **Sync with a folder** keeps places and notes the same on your devices through a folder you share between them. **Storage** shows what each file takes and can offload one, keeping its place and notes until you add it again.

A finished book can be logged in **BookShelf**, and an EPUB can be opened in **Colophon**.

_Private._ Nothing you add leaves this device unless you open a catalog, sync to a folder you chose, or share it.
